Privacy Policy

Effective November 5, 2026

Joyscape is a holiday decoration app for iPhone, iPad and Apple TV. In short: Joyscape does not collect, store or share any personal data. There is no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Information we collect

None. The app does not send any information to us or to any third party. It contains no analytics, crash-reporting or advertising software.

Information stored on your device

To remember your choices, the app keeps a few things on your device only:

  • Scenes you arrange in the editor (“My Scenes”);
  • Settings such as whether background music is on and how each scene is framed.

This information never leaves your device. Deleting the app deletes it.

Purchases

In-app purchases are handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We do not receive your name, payment details or Apple ID. The app only asks the App Store which purchases are active on your account so it can unlock them. Apple’s handling of your information is described in Apple’s Privacy Policy.

Children

Joyscape is suitable for all ages. Because it collects no data, it collects no data from children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. If a future version of the app ever needs to collect data, the policy and the app’s App Store privacy label will say so before that version is released.
